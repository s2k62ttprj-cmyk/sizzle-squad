How to play

The job

  • Orders slide in at the top. Cook them before their timer runs out.
  • Grab ingredients from crates, chop them on a board, then put them on a plate.
  • Soups: 3 chopped ingredients of one kind in a pot. Patties and fish: chop, then fry in a pan.
  • Deliver plates to the ORDER UP window. Serving the leftmost order builds a tip combo.
  • Dirty plates return to the hatch. Wash them at the sink.
  • Leave a pan too long and it burns, then catches fire. Grab the red extinguisher and hold Chop/Use to spray.

Touch

  • Drag anywhere on the left half to move.
  • GRAB picks up, puts down and combines.
  • CHOP chops, washes, and sprays the extinguisher (hold).
  • DASH gives a burst of speed.
  • Turn your phone sideways for the biggest view.

Keyboard & gamepad

  • Gollu (Player 1): W A S D move, Space grab, E chop/use, Shift dash.
  • Mokku (Player 2): Arrows move, Enter grab, Right Shift or / chop, . dash.
  • Gamepad: stick move, A grab, X chop, B dash.
  • Esc pauses.
